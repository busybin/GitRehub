<p><i>GitRehub</i> is an open-source GitHub client for Android with a modern Material 3 (Compose) interface.</p><p><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li><b>Activity feed:</b> one merged stream (received events, your own activity, and what others did to your repositories) with full filters — event type, time range, source, read state, ordering, per-author, per-repository, keyword block-list and hide-bots. Filters are saved until you clear them.</li><li><b>Repositories:</b> browse code (directories, files, branches, tags) with line numbers and in-file search, read the rendered README, follow releases and download assets, view activity and contributors.</li><li><b>Issues and pull requests:</b> native detail pages with labels, comments, reactions, assignees, milestones, reviews, and comment/close/merge actions where your permissions allow.</li><li><b>Notifications:</b> filter by type, custom swipe actions, mark all as read; read state is shared with the activity feed.</li><li><b>Search and My Work:</b> search repositories, users, issues/PRs and code; see your issues, pull requests, reviews and mentions in one tab.</li><li><b>Accounts:</b> access-token login, device-code login (with your own OAuth app), multiple accounts, profile editing.</li><li><b>App:</b> dark Material 3 with nine colour themes, four font sizes, compact or comfortable density, offline cache, opt-in update check. No ads, no trackers, no Google Play Services.</li></ul><p>Licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0.</p>
